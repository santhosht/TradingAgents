### 2. AMD Pulled Down by Broadcom's Soft AI Guidance
**Sources:** Yahoo Finance, Barrons, GuruFocus, Stocktwits News | **Dates:** 2026-06-04–05 | **Impact:** Negative (near-term)

Broadcom's (AVGO) Q2 earnings or guidance release triggered a 13% single-day decline in AVGO shares and dragged AMD, Intel, Micron, and NVIDIA lower in sympathy. Multiple headlines across different sources confirmed the read-through:

- "Intel, AMD, Micron shares trim losses after Broadcom results spark semiconductor sector sell-off" (Yahoo Finance, June 4)
- "Broadcom and Intel Stocks Drop. The Chip Selloff Isn't Done Yet." (Barrons, June 5)
- "NVDA, INTC, AMD, MU: Major Chip Stocks Fall For Second Day After AVGO's Soft AI Guidance" (Stocktwits, June 5)
- "AMD, Broadcom, Quantinuum, and More Stocks That Explain Today's Market" (Barrons, June 5)
- "Chip Selloff Hits SOX After Broadcom's 13% Drop" (GuruFocus, June 5)

**Why it matters for AMD specifically:** Broadcom and AMD serve overlapping AI infrastructure customers (hyperscalers). If Broadcom's AI guidance implies hyperscaler capex is cooling or shifting, AMD's MI300X and EPYC server ramp could face headwinds in Q2 and Q3 2026. However, a key distinction: Broadcom's AI revenue is primarily custom ASICs (TPUs for Google, AI accelerators for Meta) — a different product category than AMD's GPUs and CPUs. Analysts should not conflate the two.

**Nuance:** The same GuruFocus article noted that "options activity suggested some traders may be buying the dip" — meaning institutional players are not uniformly bearish even within the sector selloff.

**Direction:** Negative short-term (contagion). Neutral-to-positive if AMD differentiates itself at next earnings.

## News Analyst Verdict

The news environment this week presents a **classic two-layer picture:** Near-term sector headwinds from Broadcom's AI guidance disappointment, layered over a long-term structural AI narrative that remains intact for AMD. The Broadcom contagion is real but imprecise — it is being applied indiscriminately to the entire semiconductor sector when AMD's product mix (MI300X GPU, EPYC CPU) is fundamentally different from Broadcom's custom ASIC business.

The macro risks (Iran tensions, tariffs, inflation) are secondary but not negligible — they affect the rate environment and enterprise spending confidence, both of which matter for AMD's forward estimates.

The key catalyst to watch: **AMD's next quarterly earnings call**, where management will either confirm or disappoint the $13.01 forward EPS narrative. That is the single event that resolves the bullish/bearish debate definitively.
