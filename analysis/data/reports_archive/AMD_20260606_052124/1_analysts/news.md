## 5. Synthesis and Trading Implications

**Short-Term (1–2 weeks): BEARISH**  
The combination of sector contagion, macro headwinds (rising yields, strong jobs = no rate cuts), insider selling, and narrative erosion (Jensen Huang excludes AMD from "next trillion" conversation) creates a difficult near-term tape. The market is repricing AI hardware demand assumptions, and AMD carries one of the highest speculative premiums among chip stocks, making it the most vulnerable to multiple compression. The $466 level may not hold if selling pressure continues.

**Medium-Term (1–3 months): CAUTIOUSLY NEUTRAL**  
AMD's fundamental Q1 results ($10.253B revenue) are strong. The selloff is largely sentiment-and-macro-driven rather than earnings-driven. If AVGO's guidance proves conservative, or if Fed signals shift dovish, AMD could recover sharply. The analyst mean target of $482.69 provides a reference point for normalized sentiment, implying ~3.5% upside from current levels — tight, but the direction of surprise matters more than the current gap.
