## Round 1 — Bull Opens

**Pillar 1 — Real demand, real orders.** TSMC's CEO told shareholders directly: AI chip demand will exceed supply for years, even as new fabs come online. AMD's MI300X runs on TSMC's most advanced nodes. This is not a Wall Street estimate — it's a foundry CEO with direct sight into AMD's order queue confirming demand.

These aren't test pilots — they're production inference workloads at scale. Hyperscalers buy AMD for two reasons: (a) AMD MI300X has documented performance advantages in specific inference workloads, and (b) they need a second source to prevent NVIDIA monopoly pricing. Both reasons are durable.

EPYC revenue is predictable, less cyclical, and not dependent on AI GPU timing. This provides a revenue floor that the bear camp consistently ignores.

**Pillar 4 — Earnings beat history.** AMD has beaten EPS estimates for 8+ consecutive quarters. The forward EPS of $13.01 is a consensus estimate — AMD's history suggests the real number will be higher. If forward EPS comes in at $14.50, the implied price at 40× is $580, and the stock looks cheap at $542.

The revenue trajectory tells the growth story correctly when viewed YoY: Q2'25 $7.685B → Q1'26 $10.253B = +33.4%. The bear will point to sequential flatness — I'll address that in Round 2.
