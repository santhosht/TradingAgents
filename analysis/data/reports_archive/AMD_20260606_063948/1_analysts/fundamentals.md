# Fundamentals Analyst Report — AMD — AMD_20260606_063948

## Valuation

- **P/E (TTM): 156.50x** — extremely elevated. EPS TTM is only $2.98 on a $466 stock. Priced for perfection on current earnings.
- **Forward P/E: 35.67x** — dramatically lower, reflecting the market's expectation of a massive earnings inflection. Forward EPS of **$13.08** implies ~4.4x earnings growth from TTM. That is the entire bull case compressed into one ratio.
- **PEG Ratio: 1.12** — surprisingly reasonable. At this growth rate (Forward EPS $13.08 vs TTM $2.98 = ~339% growth), the PEG of 1.12 suggests the stock is not absurdly overvalued *if* the earnings ramp materializes.
- **Price/Book: 11.79x** — elevated but not extreme for a high-growth tech company.
- **EV/EBITDA: 101.21x** — most alarming metric. EBITDA TTM is $7.43B; current EBITDA annualized at Q1'26 pace = ~$9.6B. The market is paying for 3–4 years of forward EBITDA growth upfront.

## Profitability — Quarterly Trajectory

- **Revenue:** Q2'25 $7.685B → Q3'25 $9.246B → Q4'25 $10.270B → Q1'26 $10.253B. Near-flat Q4→Q1 is notable — revenue growth appears plateauing around $10.25B/quarter.
- **Gross margin:** Q1'26 = $5.416B / $10.253B = **52.8%** — consistent with TTM gross margin of 53.1%. Healthy and stable.
- **Operating income:** Q2'25 -$134M (loss) → Q3'25 $1.270B → Q4'25 $1.752B → Q1'26 $1.476B. Dramatic turnaround from loss to profitability, but sequential decline in Q1'26 vs Q4'25 is a flag.
- **Operating margin:** Q1'26 = **14.4%** — improving but modest vs Nvidia (~60%+). AMD is still scaling its operating leverage.
- **Net income:** Q1'26 $1.383B vs Q4'25 $1.511B — slight sequential decline.
- **EPS diluted:** $0.54 → $0.75 → $0.92 → $0.84 (Q2'25 through Q1'26). TTM EPS ~$3.05.

## R&D Investment

- R&D Q1'26: $2.397B vs gross profit $5.416B = **44% of gross profit to R&D**. AMD is investing massively to compete with Nvidia in AI GPUs (MI300/MI400). This constrains near-term earnings but is essential for the roadmap.

## Balance Sheet

- **Cash & ST Investments: $12.347B** (Q1'26) — up from $7.243B in Q3'25. AMD generating and retaining cash rapidly.
- **Total Debt: $3.871B** → **Net cash: +$8.476B** — excellent financial health.
- **D/E Ratio: 6.00** as reported (appears intangibles-adjusted). Actual debt/equity = $3.871B / $64.462B = ~0.06 — near-zero leverage.
- **Working Capital: $18.122B** (Q1'26) — strong liquidity.
- **Goodwill + Intangibles: $41.498B** = 52% of total assets. Primarily from Xilinx acquisition. Tangible book value $22.964B vs equity $64.462B.
- **Inventory: $8.045B** — elevated and growing ($6.677B in Q2'25). Context (revenue growth) suggests demand ramp preparation rather than softness.

## Cash Flow

- **FCF TTM: $7.17B** — strong and accelerating. Q1'26 $2.566B, Q4'25 $2.378B, Q3'25 $1.901B, Q2'25 $1.729B.
- **Capex: $389M/quarter** — fabless model keeps capital requirements low.
- **Stock comp: $487M/quarter** (~$1.95B/year) — 0.26% dilution at current market cap. Manageable.

## Growth — The Crux

- TTM EPS: $2.98. Forward EPS: $13.08. Implied EPS growth: **~339%**.
- The quarterly EPS trend ($0.54 → $0.75 → $0.92 → $0.84) does NOT yet show the inflection needed to reach $13.08 annualized (~$3.27/quarter sustained). Q1'26 EPS was $0.84.
- The market is pricing in H2'26 EPS of ~$4–$5+ per quarter. This is the key execution risk — AMD must more than triple quarterly earnings from current levels.
