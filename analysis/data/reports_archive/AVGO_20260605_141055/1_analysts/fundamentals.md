## Overview

Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) is a $1.89 trillion market cap semiconductor and infrastructure software company. Following its June 4 earnings-driven selloff, the stock now trades at $418.91 — representing a significant valuation reset from the $479.23 prior close. This report evaluates whether the current valuation is justified, stretched, or offers value relative to the company's actual growth trajectory.

## Valuation

### P/E Analysis: The Compression Opportunity

| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| P/E (TTM) | 66.80× | Elevated for semis; justified only by growth acceleration |
| Forward P/E | 20.90× | Dramatically cheaper — pricing in massive EPS expansion |
| PEG Ratio | 1.05 | Fair value territory for high-growth AI |
| Price/Book | 21.66× | High; reflects intangible asset-heavy post-acquisition balance sheet |
| EV/EBITDA | 48.48× | Premium; requires sustained high-margin growth |

The most striking feature of AVGO's valuation is the gap between TTM P/E (66.80×) and Forward P/E (20.90×). This 3.2× compression ratio implies the market expects EPS to nearly triple from $5.99 (TTM) to $19.14 (Forward). This is an extraordinary implied earnings inflection — and is the single most important number in the whole analysis.

**Is the $19.14 Forward EPS credible?** Looking at the quarterly trajectory:
- Q2 FY2025: EPS $1.03
- Q3 FY2025: EPS $0.85 (dip)
- Q4 FY2025: EPS $1.74
- Q1 FY2026: EPS $1.50

Annualizing Q1 FY2026 run rate: $1.50 × 4 = $6.00/share — this matches TTM EPS of $5.99. The forward EPS of $19.14 implies a dramatic acceleration from the current $6.00/year run rate. This requires AI revenue to scale 3× in the forward period. That is the bull case embedded in the valuation — and it is what markets will scrutinize most harshly given the Q2 software revenue miss.

The **PEG of 1.05** is genuinely compelling: it signals that if the growth embedded in Forward EPS actually materializes, AVGO is trading at roughly fair value right now after the selloff. This is a key bull anchor.

| Metric | Q1 FY2026 | Assessment |

| Cash & ST Investments | $14.17B | Substantial liquidity buffer |

| Tangible Book Value | −$48.23B | Negative; reflects acquisition premium |

The $64.9B in total debt is the bear's most credible concern. However, the debt must be evaluated against AVGO's FCF generation:

- Q1 FY2026 FCF: $8.01B
- Q4 FY2025 FCF: $7.47B
- Q3 FY2025 FCF: $7.02B
- Q2 FY2025 FCF: $6.41B

Trailing 4-quarter FCF: approximately $28.9B, consistent with the reported $27.21B TTM FCF. At this pace, AVGO could theoretically repay its entire debt within ~2.3 years from FCF alone — though it instead balances debt service, buybacks, and dividends. The working capital improvement from $1.6B (Q2 FY2025) to $15.2B (Q1 FY2026) is a notable positive signal: the balance sheet is strengthening rapidly.

The **negative tangible book value** (−$48.23B) is a structural feature of acquisition-driven companies and is not itself alarming for a company generating $8B+/quarter in cash. However, it means that in a severe AI narrative reversal, goodwill impairment charges could be significant.

## Cash Flow: Exceptional and Accelerating

| Metric | Q1 FY2026 | Q4 FY2025 | Q3 FY2025 | Q2 FY2025 |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Operating CF | $8.26B | $7.70B | $7.17B | $6.56B |
| Capex | −$250M | −$237M | −$142M | −$144M |
| FCF | $8.01B | $7.47B | $7.02B | $6.41B |

FCF has grown from $6.41B to $8.01B in four quarters — a 25% increase in FCF in one year. This is remarkable for a company of this scale. Capital expenditure remains remarkably low ($250M vs. $8B+ operating CF), reflecting AVGO's fabless/asset-light model. The company converts 97% of operating cash flow to free cash flow — a ratio typical of software companies, not semiconductor manufacturers.

Stock buybacks of $7.85B in Q1 FY2026 (with nothing in Q4 FY2025) represent an aggressive but inconsistent capital return posture. When combined with dividends ($62% yield data suggests a reporting artifact — the actual dividend yield is likely ~2.0–2.5% at current price), AVGO is returning substantial capital to shareholders.

**Conclusion:** At $418.91 with a Forward P/E of 20.90× and a PEG of 1.05, AVGO is fairly valued if the earnings acceleration is real. The stock is NOT cheap in absolute terms — it requires execution. The $19.14 Forward EPS is achievable given the Q1 FY2026 operational trajectory, but requires a re-acceleration in AI software revenue recognition that the Q2 miss has put in doubt for one quarter.

The fundamentals are genuinely strong. This is a company with 76% gross margins, $8B/quarter in FCF, and revenue growing at 7–13% per quarter. The valuation is premium but not irrational for a company of this quality with this AI exposure. The risk is not the business — it is the expectations gap between the $19.14 Forward EPS and the current software revenue run rate.
