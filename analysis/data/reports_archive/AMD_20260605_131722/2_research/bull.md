The question isn't whether Barrons is bearish; it's whether the fundamental thesis has changed. It has not. TSMC demand is still exceeding supply. When one hyperscaler finishes its MI300X deployment, another picks up the slack. That's the difference between a single-customer dependency (AVGO) and a multi-customer product (AMD).

This confirms that AI chip demand is physical and contracted, not hypothetical. If HBM is sold out and AMD uses HBM in its MI300X, AMD's production is limited by supply, not by demand. **The AMD upgrade today:** When a stock is under sector-wide selling pressure and an analyst specifically upgrades AMD for AI growth, that analyst is saying: "The market is wrong to paint AMD with the AVGO brush. AMD's fundamentals are different." I agree with that analyst.
