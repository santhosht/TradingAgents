## 1. Trend Overview

AMD closed at $466.38 on June 5, 2026 — a brutal -10.86% single-day decline on volume of 46.7M shares (significantly above the ~30M average of the prior two weeks). This drop reverses a powerful rally that had carried AMD from $393.36 (May 19 low) to a 52-week high of $546.44 (June 3), a 38.9% move in just 11 trading days.

The stock is now at an inflection point. The selloff was sector-wide (Nasdaq -4.2%, chip-specific: NVDA -5.9%), triggered by the May jobs report beating expectations (pushing bond yields higher), AVGO earnings overhang, and concerns around AI chip export restrictions to China.
