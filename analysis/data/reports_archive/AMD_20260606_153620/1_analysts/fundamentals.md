## 1. Valuation

| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| P/E TTM | 156.50× | Elevated; reflects market pricing explosive forward growth |
| Forward P/E | 35.67× | Reasonable for high-growth semis if EPS delivery occurs |
| PEG Ratio | 1.12 | Nearly fair-valued on a PEG basis — implies ~31% EPS growth is priced in |
| Price/Book | 11.79× | Premium; reflects Xilinx intangibles ($41.5B goodwill) and AI optionality |
| EV/EBITDA | 101.21× | Very high on TTM EBITDA; reflective of growth expectations |

The TTM P/E of 156.50× is a headline risk number but misleading as a standalone. The forward P/E at 35.67× is the operative metric — it implies the market believes AMD can grow EPS from $2.98 TTM to $13.08 forward, a 4.4× EPS expansion. That expansion is the crux of the bull thesis. The PEG ratio of 1.12 suggests the market is paying fair (slightly premium) value for AMD's expected growth rate — not egregiously overvalued on a growth-adjusted basis.

**EPS Inflection Check:** Q1 2026 EPS of $0.84 (diluted) vs. prior quarters shows strong sequential acceleration. The forward EPS of $13.08 implies roughly $3.27/quarter average — AMD is at $0.84 in Q1, meaning significant back-half 2026 ramp is priced in. This is the key execution risk.

- Q2 2025: Revenue $7.685B → Q3: $9.246B → Q4: $10.270B → Q1 2026: $10.253B
- Revenue growth from Q2 2025 to Q1 2026: +33.4% in 3 quarters

The Q1 2026 gross profit dip ($5.416B vs. Q4's $5.577B) is a yellow flag — revenue was roughly flat but gross profit declined. R&D spending at $2.397B in Q1 2026 — AMD is investing aggressively, which should pay off in future product cycles but compresses current margins.
