## The Aggressive Analyst's Math Is Flawed

"9% position × $78 stop / $466 = 1.5% portfolio loss if stopped." Most professional risk managers cap single-trade drawdown at 0.5–1.0% of portfolio. The Aggressive Analyst argued for 1.5× the professional maximum drawdown on a single position.
