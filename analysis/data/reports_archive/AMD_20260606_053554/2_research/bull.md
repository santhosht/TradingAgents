## Direct Rebuttals to Every Bear Round 1 Claim

---

### Bear Claim 1: "TTM P/E 156× and EV/EBITDA 101× indefensible"

The bear is making a classic mistake: valuing a growth-inflection company on **backward-looking earnings**. TTM P/E on a company in the middle of a revenue ramp is like judging Amazon in 2015 on its then-negative operating margin. It tells you where AMD *was*, not where it is going.

The market does not price AMD on TTM EPS of $2.98. It prices AMD on **forward EPS of $13.08**. At $466.38 and $13.08 forward EPS, the Forward P/E is **35.67×** — not 156×. A 35.67× forward multiple for a company growing revenue at 33% YoY and generating $7.17B in annual free cash flow is not only defensible, it is *conservative* for an AI infrastructure secular winner.

On EV/EBITDA: Q1 2026 quarterly EBITDA was $2.40B — annualized run rate of **$9.6B**, making NTM EV/EBITDA approximately **78×**, not 101×. And with EBITDA expanding every quarter, by Q4 2026 the EV/EBITDA compresses further. The bear is anchoring on a stale denominator.

---

### Bear Claim 2: "Forward EPS $13.08 requires 4× earnings jump — moonshot"

Q1 2026 EPS was $0.84. But look at the **trajectory**:
- Q2 2025: $0.54 EPS
- Q3 2025: $0.75 EPS
- Q4 2025: $0.92 EPS
- Q1 2026: $0.84 EPS

That is a **56% EPS increase** in just three quarters ($0.54 → $0.84). Operating cash flow grew from $2.01B to $2.96B over the same period — **47% increase in four quarters**. The operating leverage machinery is clearly working.

The path to $13.08 requires: (1) data center GPU mix shift — MI300X/MI400 carry ~55–60% gross margins; (2) operating leverage — R&D at $2.397B does not need to grow proportionally as revenue scales; (3) EPYC market share gains continuing against Intel. The bear's "$3.36 annualized run rate" ignores the steep operating leverage ramp built into AMD's product cycle and TSMC N3 process advantages in MI400.
