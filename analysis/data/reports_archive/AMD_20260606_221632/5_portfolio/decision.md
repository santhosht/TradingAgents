# Portfolio Manager — Final Decision — AMD — 2026-06-06

## Synthesis

After the full pipeline — four analyst reports, three rounds of adversarial Bull/Bear debate, the Research Manager's verdict, the Trader's proposal, and the Aggressive/Conservative/Neutral risk panel — the evidence is clear on the direction and requires calibrated judgment on sizing and entry.

**Business quality:** Exceptional. $7.17B FCF TTM growing 48%, $8.48B net cash, $37.45B revenue, Forward P/E 35.67×, PEG 1.12. This is one of the two GPU franchise companies in the AI infrastructure cycle. The business did not change on June 5–6.

**Selloff character:** Macro-driven. The Nasdaq dropped 4.2% on a jobs report and AVGO earnings. AMD, at Beta 2.49, amplified the move to -17% in 48 hours. No analyst downgraded AMD. TD Cowen raised the PT to $600 post-management meeting during the actual selloff.

**Legitimate concerns:** EV/EBITDA 101× is genuinely stretched; the Bull's DCF produced ~$202B below current market cap on conservative assumptions. Q1'26 shows sequential: operating income -$276M, gross margin -150bps, revenue flat, inventory +20.5%. These are monitoring flags, not fatal flaws.

**Risk panel verdict:** Aggressive says max position; Conservative says wait; Neutral says 3.5–4% with modified stop. The Neutral analyst's corrections to the Trader's plan — stop at $410, time exit by July 20 — are sound and adopted.

## Final Rating: **Overweight**

AMD at $466.38 is a fundamentally strong AI infrastructure compounder undergoing a temporary macro repricing. The stock is not cheap on EV/EBITDA (101×) but is reasonably valued on the metrics most relevant to its growth phase: Forward P/E 35.67× and PEG 1.12. The 17% pullback from $546 has created a better risk-reward entry than existed two weeks ago.

The research outcome is Overweight, not Buy, because: (a) the $202B DCF gap requires execution assumptions that carry real risk, (b) near-term technicals are still negative (MACD bearish crossover, below EMA 10, RSI 58 with room to fall), and (c) Q1'26 sequential signals warrant one more quarter of confirmation before maximum conviction is warranted.
