# NVDA Conservative Risk Analysis — June 6, 2026

## Thesis

The aggressive analyst has built a compelling fundamental case on a stock that is, right now, in active technical deterioration. NVDA's business is exceptional — PEG 0.66, forward P/E 16.17x, $194B annualized FCF — but the aggressive argument conflates business quality with entry timing. That conflation is precisely how disciplined investors get hurt in otherwise correct long-term calls.

Sizing up to 2.5% risk at $204–$207 immediately is dangerous not because NVDA is a bad stock, but because the current technical setup has not yet produced the confirmation signals that justify committing full capital at elevated risk. MFI being oversold (28.92) is a precondition for a bounce, not a guarantee of one. At Beta 2.20, a 5% further decline to $194 from entry at $206 is routine — and at 2.5% sizing, that drawdown is difficult to recover from psychologically and mechanically.

The 9.2:1 R:R to T2 requires holding 9–12 months through a wall of overhead supply and through at least two earnings cycles at Beta 2.20.

The June 1 bounce of +6.26% on 212.9M shares was completely reversed by June 5 — a textbook failed recovery. No confirmation yet that the distribution sequence is over.
