**Bear wins:**

**Draw/Contested:**
- China export risk: real but proportionality uncertain
- Inventory build: legitimate medium-term risk, not yet confirmed cycle turn
- "50% datacenter cancellations": unconfirmed rumor; bear loses points for relying on this without data file support

**Debate winner:** Bear wins near-term risk/reward; Bull wins fundamental quality and long-term thesis.
