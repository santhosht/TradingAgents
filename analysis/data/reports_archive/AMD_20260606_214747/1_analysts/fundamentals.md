# Fundamentals Report — AMD — 2026-06-06

---

## Valuation

- **P/E TTM: 156.50×** — Extreme; priced on forward earnings, not current earnings.
- **Forward P/E: 35.67×** — The critical number. Forward EPS of $13.08 implies 4.4× earnings increase from TTM.
- **PEG Ratio: 1.12** — Most constructive signal. Near 1.0 = fairly valued relative to growth rate.
- **Price/Book: 11.79×** — High; Xilinx goodwill ($41.5B) understates economic assets.
- **EV/EBITDA: 101.21×** — TTM basis; forward basis ~40–50× given Q1'26 EBITDA of $2.4B ($9.6B annualized).

Key question: is Forward EPS of $13.08 achievable? Q1'26 EPS of $0.84 requires ~4× to reach $3.27/quarter annualized. The ramp requires Data Center GPU acceleration in H2'26.

## Profitability

Revenue trajectory (exceptional):
- Q2'25: $7.685B → Q3'25: $9.246B → Q4'25: $10.270B → Q1'26: $10.253B
- ~33% YoY revenue growth; TTM: $37.45B

Gross margin expansion (most important trend):
- Q2'25: 39.8% → Q3'25: 51.7% → Q4'25: 54.3% → Q1'26: 52.8%
- Product mix shift to high-margin GPU/AI accelerators is clear

Operating margins:
- Q2'25: -1.7% → Q3'25: 13.7% → Q4'25: 17.1% → Q1'26: 14.4%
- High R&D ($2.4B in Q1'26) consuming gross profit expansion. Q1'26 sequential dip from Q4'25 worth monitoring.

Net Income: Q2'25: $872M → Q3'25: $1.243B → Q4'25: $1.511B → Q1'26: $1.383B (sequential dip)
ROE: 8.1% | ROA: 3.6% — modest but inflated by $41.5B goodwill base

## Balance Sheet

- **Cash & ST Investments: $12.35B** (up from $5.87B in Q2'25 — more than doubled in 4 quarters)
- **Total Debt: $3.87B** — Net cash position: **$8.48B**
- **D/E Ratio: 6.00** — Misleading; $41.5B goodwill from Xilinx distorts equity base. Tangible book $22.96B; debt is conservative on practical basis.
- **Current Ratio: 2.73** — Very healthy liquidity
- **Working Capital: $18.12B** — Ample
- **Inventory: $8.045B** — Growing; monitor for demand/supply imbalance

## Cash Flow

- **Operating CF:** Q1'26: $2.955B, Q4'25: $2.600B — consistent sequential improvement; $10.7B annualized
- **FCF TTM: $7.17B** — Strong; FCF yield ~0.94% at $760B market cap
- **Capex: $389M** Q1'26 (3.8% of revenue) — Fabless model keeps capex low
- **Stock buybacks: $355M** Q1'26; stock comp: $487M — roughly offsetting

## Growth Analysis

EPS inflection from TTM $2.98 to Forward $13.08 (+339%) is the central thesis. Quarterly progression (Q2'25: $0.54 → Q3'25: $0.75 → Q4'25: $0.92 → Q1'26: $0.84) shows growth but not yet at the pace implied. Data center GPU ramp (MI300 series and successors) is the primary driver.

## Analyst Consensus

- Mean target: $482.69 (3.5% upside from $466)
- High target: $665.00 (42.6% upside)
- Low target: $225.00 (51.8% downside)
- Recommendation: **STRONG_BUY**

Mean analyst target just above current price — AMD is now trading near consensus after the pullback. TD Cowen's $600 target (above mean) suggests consensus drifting higher.

## Valuation Verdict

AMD is in genuine hypergrowth: 33% revenue growth, dramatic gross margin expansion, compounding FCF, fortress balance sheet. The TTM P/E of 156× is not the right lens — PEG 1.12 and Forward P/E 35.67× are. Key risk: if AI data center spend slows or Nvidia captures most incremental opportunity, forward earnings don't materialize and multiple collapses.
