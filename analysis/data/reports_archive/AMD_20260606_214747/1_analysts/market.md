Any meaningful retest of SMA 50 would require a decline to ~$358, a further 23% drop from current levels — this is the "disaster" scenario, not the base case.

While not a short-term timing signal, it reinforces that the stock has traveled a long distance quickly and is vulnerable to extended corrections.
