## Balanced Independent Assessment

### Where the Aggressive Analyst Overreaches

"Capitulation = green light" conflates technical exhaustion signals with a clear reversal. RSI 34.33 and MFI 28.92 are oversold but can persist when structural headwinds are genuine. The H20 restriction is not noise — $15B is ~6% of $253.49B TTM revenue, permanently impaired. The 5-to-1 R/R claim depends entirely on analyst target $298.07, set before H20 restrictions were finalized. Using a pre-impairment target to justify maximum sizing is circular. Stevens $221M insider buy is material but one purchase does not override accelerating MACD histogram, widening distribution, and the May 14 peak pattern.

### Where the Conservative Analyst Overstates

Inventory build (+72% to $25.80B) carries weight but lacks context: NVDA is actively ramping Blackwell while managing Hopper end-of-life. Supply-constrained companies in hypergrowth carry elevated inventory during transitions. 2022 comparison had a different cause — demand evaporated suddenly after a consumer GPU bubble. Current inventory build coincides with $48.59B FCF/quarter and $68B net cash — not the 2022 balance sheet profile.

Conservative analyst also understates the valuation case: Forward P/E 16.17x with +93.9% EPS growth and PEG 0.66 is genuinely cheap on a growth-adjusted basis. P/B 31.69x is largely irrelevant for an asset-light, software-gross-margin semiconductor business with 74.1% gross margin and $68B net cash. Using the wrong metric to justify excessive caution weakens the argument.

### Independent Assessment

**Broadcom falling on beat-and-raise is the most important data point** — it signals multiple compression across the AI infrastructure trade, not individual execution failure. That is a multiple compression event, not fundamental deterioration. Multiple compression in a Beta 2.20 name during risk-off rotation = current price action confirmed.

**Structural case intact but impaired.** Revenue +74%, FCF $48.59B/quarter, $68B net cash, Computex catalysts, and $920M/month GPU contracts confirm demand isn't collapsing. H20 restriction is real but custom silicon threat (TPU v5, Trainium2, Maia 2, MTIA) is a 3–5 year slow-moving risk, not a Q3 revenue event. Overstating near-term custom silicon risk weakens the conservative argument.

**VWMA $218.60 is the number to watch.** Price 6.5% below VWMA confirms active distribution. No durable rally while sitting this far below VWMA unless volume pattern shifts decisively.
